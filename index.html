Model assumptions and limitations

Arrivals are evenly spaced, per-attempt latency is constant, failure probability is independent outside a fixed outage, and outage membership is determined at attempt start. Every started attempt incurs the entered price, even on failure or deadline expiry. Backoff is capped exponential; deadlines include arrival-to-completion time and retry waits. The circuit opens after consecutive completed failures, not after rejected calls.

P95 “all” includes failed-fast requests; a lower value may reflect more rejections, not healthier service. Successful-request P95 is shown separately. No rate limits, queue capacity, service saturation, token-varying costs, Retry-After, cancellation propagation, correlated failures beyond the outage or real idempotency semantics are modeled. These numbers are design experiments, not predictions.
